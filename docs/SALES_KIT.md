# Sales kit

Do not say the preview is exact. Do not mention another salon's private tool as if it were ours.

## Pitch

"Guests already ask for Korean layers and cherry red from Instagram. This page shows a colour on their phone, without uploading it, and a cut preview they can send you on WhatsApp with the services and prices filled in. You do not build an app. We put your name, your colours, and your menu on a link and a QR for the mirror."

One colour ticket in a month is the story for Starter. Confirm the salon's real colour price before you use that line. **[Assumption: many colour tickets are ₹2,000 or more.]**

## One-pager

**HelixStac TryOn** — your brand, their phone.

- Live colour, 16 shades, free, on the phone.
- Cut preview, about 10 seconds, a guide for the stylist.
- Book opens WhatsApp with the look and your prices.
- English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi.
- Starter ₹799/mo, Pro ₹1,999/mo, Chain ₹4,999/mo for 3 outlets. Ex-GST, plus 18% GST. Annual is two months free.
- 14-day trial. When preview credits run out, colour still works.

## Demo script (12 minutes)

1. Open `/s/demo-salon` on your phone, then on theirs.
2. Read the consent in one sentence: colour stays here, the cut preview is not saved.
3. Camera, Cherry Red, intensity. Say it is a tint, not a balayage.
4. Women's Soft Bob or Men's Mid Fade. Wait. Use the before/after slider.
5. Read the disclaimer out loud.
6. Tap Book. Show the WhatsApp draft: look, colour, Haircut / colour / styling prices.
7. Show the admin: credits left, the lead row, the QR.
8. Ask which three styles they would turn off. Turn them off if you are in their trial salon.

## Objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "AI hair looks fake." | "It is a guide. If it does not look like the guest, they still have live colour and they still WhatsApp you. We do not promise the cut." |
| "My suite is ₹499." | "That suite books appointments. This is the conversation before the colour service. One extra colour client covers the month." |
| "I don't want photos stored." | "Live colour never uploads. The cut preview is processed and not saved. You can read that on the privacy page. Consent is logged without the photo." |
| "My clients are not on apps." | "They scan a QR. No install. Hindi or Kannada is on the same page." |
| "What if credits finish on a Saturday?" | "Colour keeps working. The preview button explains that credits are used up and points them to WhatsApp." |
